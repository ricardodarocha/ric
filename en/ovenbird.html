SECTION 01

BIOMIMETICS & URBAN SYMBIOSIS

Urbanization and climate change have severely altered traditional riverfront dynamics. Flash flooding and river surges require non-intrusive, continuous surveillance.

Inspired by the Rufous Hornero (João-de-Barro), which constructs resilient mud nests on street poles and structures, our luminaire doubles as a smart sensor station and an artificial nesting habitat for indigenous bird species.

ECOLOGICAL ADVANTAGES
  • Restores native avian nesting habitats in urban centers.
  • Non-interfering thermal isolation between nest and electronics.
  • 100% weather-resistant ceramic and recycled composite housing.
